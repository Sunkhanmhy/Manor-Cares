    Frequently Asked Questions

    What tasks are included in a standard cleaning? +

    A standard clean covers dusting surfaces, wiping counters, vacuuming floors, mopping hard floors, cleaning sinks, and taking out the trash.

    Are your cleaning workers insured and vetted? +

    Yes, we conducted a deep background checks on all our workers. They also carry insurance to cover any accidental damage to your home.

    Do I need to provide cleaning supplies and tools? +

    No! We bring our own tools and eco-friendly products. If you want us to use your special product, just leave it out and ask us.

    Can I request a same-week appointment? +

    Yes, subject to our availability, schedule and fixed appointments. Mention your preferred date in the message field above.

    How do you calculate the cleaning price? +

    Prices depend on the size of your property, the number of rooms, and the type of cleaning you need. For more details, contact our customer care representative.

    What is your cancellation policy? +

    Our services let you cancel or change your time slot for free if you give 24 hours notice. Late cancellations may incur a small fee.

    What happens if I am not happy with the cleaning?+

    Refunds available, with minimal discount. Also, we offer a re-cleaning service to ensure your satisfaction.
